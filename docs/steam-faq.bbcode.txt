=== THREAD 1 — title: "FAQ — read before asking" ===

[b]Is it free?[/b]
The mod is free (public domain, even). The AI usually isn't — you bring your own API key (OpenRouter by default and recommended) and put a few bucks on it. [b]Two free roads exist:[/b] a [b]Gemini[/b] key from aistudio.google.com has a real free tier (~1,500 replies a day, no card — but Google trains on free-tier traffic so it isn't private, and it's slow: its thinking can't be switched off), or a local model on your own machine — tinkerers only, unsupported; see below. No key and no local model, no talking NPCs.

[b]OK, but how much does it actually cost?[/b]
A typical exchange is about a tenth of a cent on the default — $10 covers many thousands of messages. The mod shows each interaction's tokens and price as you play, keeps daily totals, and has an optional hard daily cap (MaxDailyRequests) so it can never run away from you. Your key, your bill, your eyes on it.

[b]Can I use OpenRouter / NanoGPT / a local LLM?[/b]
All of them. [b]OpenRouter is the default and the one I recommend[/b]: pick it in Mod Options, paste one key from openrouter.ai, use [b]openai/gpt-5.6-luna[/b] (tuned and tested against) or [b]openai/gpt-5.4-mini[/b] as the proven fallback. OpenAI, Gemini, DeepSeek and Claude each have their own backend too; Grok and Mistral ride the router, and any other id can be typed in — at your own risk, and duller without native tool calling. Other OpenAI-compatible services (NanoGPT…) connect through [b]Custom endpoint[/b]: paste the base URL ending in /v1, put that service's key in the OpenAI key field, set its model id in config.json. The connection check at campaign start tells you plainly whether your setup works.

[b]Local models — what to expect?[/b]
[b]Tinkerers only, at your own risk[/b] — local exists because people asked, not because it plays well. You own the setup, and I don't debug local setups. Still want to? Pick [b]Local[/b] in Mod Options, run [b]LM Studio[/b] (the default, localhost:1234) or [b]Ollama[/b] (paste http://localhost:11434/v1 as the local server URL), set the exact model id your server serves and the context length you loaded it with. Free, private, nothing leaves your PC. The short rules:
[list]
[*]An [b]instruct[/b] model with native tool calling — Qwen3.6-35B-A3B, GPT-OSS-20B, Mistral Small 24B. 8B-class models talk but fumble the tools.
[*][b]Thinking/reasoning OFF[/b] in your server, or replies arrive as "…" — the model spent its whole budget thinking. log.txt says so plainly when it happens.
[*][b]12–16+ GB VRAM[/b] and 32 GB RAM, [b]16k+ context[/b] mirrored in the mod's Local context length setting. Below that, stay on the cloud backends.
[*]Replies are [b]slow[/b] — live in the chat window (hotkey [b]O[/b]); the face-to-face panel feels broken on the wait. If relations never move, set RelationshipChangesViaTool to false.
[/list]
Full setup guide, and how local compares to the cloud options: [url=https://github.com/TraxData313/ImmersiveAI/blob/master/docs/choosing-a-model.md]Which AI should I use?[/url]

[b]Where does the mod actually live on my disk? Where do I put my key?[/b]
Two places, and the split matters:
[list]
[*]The mod's own files (code, never yours to edit): Steam puts Workshop mods under [b]steamapps\workshop\content\261550\3764210301[/b] inside your Steam folder — not in the game's Modules folder like manual installs. You never need to touch it; Steam replaces it on every update.
[*]Everything YOURS (config.json with your key, all character memories, letters, logs): [b]Documents\Mount and Blade II Bannerlord\Configs\ImmersiveAI[/b] — created the first time you run the mod. This is the only folder you ever open, and Steam never touches it.
[/list]

[b]If I uninstall and reinstall, do the characters still remember me? What about another PC?[/b]
Uninstall/reinstall on the same machine: yes, everything persists. Unsubscribing only removes the mod's code — your key, every memory and every letter stay in the Documents folder above, and resubscribing picks them right back up.
Another PC: not automatically. Steam Cloud syncs your Bannerlord saves, but NOT the mod's Documents folder — on a new machine the characters would wake up blank. To move, copy [b]Documents\Mount and Blade II Bannerlord\Configs\ImmersiveAI[/b] over yourself (it's all plain files). Mind that config.json inside carries your API key — treat the copy like a password.

[b]My NPCs are silent / answer nothing. Help?[/b]
[olist]
[*]Read the startup message. When a campaign loads, the mod tests your connection and says plainly what it found — "connected", a missing/wrong key, or a network problem — and where to fix it. Fix config.json, restart, the check runs again.
[*]Check Documents\Mount and Blade II Bannerlord\Configs\ImmersiveAI\log.txt — every call and every error, in plain words.
[*]A wrong or dead key shows ONE amber notice, then goes quiet instead of erroring hourly. Fix the key and everything comes back.
[/olist]
Still stuck? Post the last lines of log.txt in the Bug reports thread — the mod never logs your key.

[b]How do I get the voices working? "No speech engine installed"[/b]
Voices are [b]off by default[/b] and need a speech engine on your own PC — a real ~2.9 GB download, and it wants an [b]NVIDIA[/b] card. The engine ships only as a CUDA build, so there is no AMD, Intel or CPU road. Open the talk screen (O) -> [b]Voices[/b] -> [b]Download the voices[/b], say yes, and it fetches everything in the background while you play; then [b]Turn voices on[/b] and press the play mark beside any line.
No download button, or nothing happens when you press it? The panel says which of three it is: everything is already installed, the machine has no NVIDIA card, or the mod's own voice helper is missing from its folder (reinstalling Immersive AI puts it back). log.txt records where it looked.
[b]No NVIDIA card?[/b] Put an OpenAI key into [b]Voices -> Hosted voices: API key[/b] instead — thirteen voices appear at once, nothing is downloaded, nothing touches your card, about 1.5 cents a minute of speech. Full guide: [url=https://github.com/TraxData313/ImmersiveAI/blob/master/docs/voiceover-setup.md]Hearing them speak[/url]

[b]Which model should I pick?[/b]
[b]gpt-5.6-luna[/b] (via OpenRouter as openai/gpt-5.6-luna, or straight from OpenAI) — the default and what the mod is tuned and tested against, and 80% cheaper since July 2026. Want it free: [b]Gemini + gemini-3.6-flash[/b] (slow — its thinking can't be off). Want the smallest bill: [b]DeepSeek + deepseek-v4-flash[/b]. Claude works but isn't tested at length. Everything else is at your own risk and must carry native tool calling. Going local: Qwen3.6-35B-A3B (instruct), thinking turned off. Full comparison: [url=https://github.com/TraxData313/ImmersiveAI/blob/master/docs/choosing-a-model.md]Which AI should I use?[/url]

[b]Where do my conversations go? (privacy)[/b]
To the AI provider YOU configured, under YOUR key and their terms — nowhere else (and with the Local backend, they never leave your machine at all). With web search on (default), characters may look things up mid-reply; those queries go to DuckDuckGo — turn EnableWebSearch off to keep everything between you and your provider. The mod itself collects no telemetry whatsoever. All memories are plain files on your own disk, yours to read, edit, or delete.

[b]Can I add it to an existing campaign? Remove it mid-campaign?[/b]
Yes and yes. Existing saves work. Removing is safe at any time — even a save carrying a pending Immersive AI notice loads fine without the mod (the game quietly discards it; you'll just see the usual "different modules" warning every modded save shows). Memories live outside the saves and survive either way — reinstall later and every character remembers you.

[b]Why do my NPCs remember things a reload should have undone?[/b]
They don't — each save photographs their memories and loading rewinds them with it (RevertMemoriesWithSaves, on by default). A reload truly un-remembers a bad turn.

[b]Nobody ever approaches me or writes to me. Broken?[/b]
Probably just honest: a fresh game is quiet because nobody knows you yet. Approaches scale with real history — how much you've talked, how strongly they feel about you, how recently you spoke. Raise the on-map SOCIALNESS dial to invite more (0 = leave me be), spend time talking, and give letters a few in-game days to travel. Keeps in mind: people also sleep at night.

[b]Compatibility — game version, other mods, requirements?[/b]
[list]
[*][b]Game version:[/b] built and tested on the current live version (v1.4.7). Older game versions are untested and unlikely to work — the mod uses current APIs.
[*][b]Requires:[/b] nothing but the base game and an AI to think with — your API key, or a local model. Harmony comes bundled inside the mod — no separate Harmony, ButterLib or UIExtenderEx downloads. MCM (Mod Configuration Menu) is optional: with it you get an in-game settings menu, without it everything is set in config.json.
[*][b]Other mods:[/b] it replaces no vanilla behavior (one light Harmony postfix is all), so it coexists peacefully with overhauls and most everything else. Characters added by other mods get minds like everyone else — personalities are built from live game data, whoever put them in the world.
[*][b]War Sails:[/b] neither required nor conflicting — sea-battles even get their own entries in the battle chronicle. [b]Marry Anyone:[/b] detected automatically; with it installed an existing marriage no longer bars a new courtship, and its rules stay the judge. [b]Training Battles:[/b] drills are never mistaken for real war — nothing enters the chronicle.
[*][b]Load order:[/b] nothing to arrange — the launcher sorts it automatically (after the game modules, and after MCM if you have it).
[*][b]Singleplayer only.[/b]
[/list]
Adding to an existing campaign or removing mid-campaign is covered above — both are safe.

[b]Can I speak my own language?[/b]
Yes — speak anything to them and they answer in kind. The mod's own UI (windows, notices) is English for now.

[b]Can I actually marry an NPC? Court one?[/b]
Yes, and it is the heart of v2.0. It happens in conversation: their heart walks its own road (liking, love, readiness, betrothal, wedding), moved by their own judgment of your talks. When marriage truly enters the talk they write down what honestly worries them about a life together, in their own words — talk those worries over; only they decide when life has answered one. Nothing is sealed by words alone: the betrothal and the wedding each take a confirming click from you, and the wedding is the real game marriage (cutscene, clan, children). A noble bride's family asks a bride-price, haggled in talk or by letter. Companion brides and grooms work too — vanilla forbids them; here they are the point. Toggle it all with "Marriage by courtship" in Mod Options.

[b]How is this different from ChatAi?[/b]
ChatAi inspired this mod and is a good free way to taste the idea. Immersive AI is a clean-room rewrite (no code copied) built around what ChatAi doesn't do: persistent layered memory that survives long conversations, NPCs with their own moods, voices and starting personality, native tool use (they look things up instead of hallucinating), NPCs that approach YOU and write letters, a chronicle of every battle you fought together, courtship and marriage, hiring by handshake, a chat window and a letter window. The trade: you bring your own key (or a local model and the machine to run it).

[b]Can I shape the world or individual characters?[/b]
Yes — plain text files under Documents\...\Configs\ImmersiveAI\: global_prompt.txt colors every mind in the world; each character's folder has custom_instructions.txt for personal secrets and quirks (write it in their own voice — "I stutter when I am nervous"), plus their memories, self-image, battles and letters as readable text. Both prompts are also editable from inside the game, in the chat and letter windows. Changes apply on the next conversation, no restart.

[b]Where exactly are a character's memories? How do I wipe them?[/b]
Everything a character remembers lives in [b]Documents\Mount and Blade II Bannerlord\Configs\ImmersiveAI\NPCs\campaign_<id>\<character>\[/b] as plain files: memories.json (what they remember of you), self.txt (who they've become), custom_instructions.txt (their own prompt, including the starting personality written for them), letters.txt (your correspondence). Close the game, then delete what you want gone:
[list]
[*]One file — e.g. delete memories.json and they forget your history but keep their sense of self.
[*]A character's whole folder — they meet you as a stranger again (their self re-seeds from the story the world tells of them).
[*]A whole campaign_* folder — resets that entire playthrough's memories.
[/list]
One catch: each save carries a photograph of the memories (that's what makes reloading honest), so loading an older save can bring wiped memories back. If you want the wipe to stick everywhere, also delete the campaign folder's _snapshots subfolder — or just wipe and save fresh.

=== THREAD 2 — title: "Bug reports" ===

Found something broken? Post it here — I'm grateful for every report.

Please include:
[olist]
[*]The last ~20 lines of Documents\Mount and Blade II Bannerlord\Configs\ImmersiveAI\log.txt (it records every call and error in plain words, and it NEVER contains your key or your conversations — safe to paste).
[*]Your backend and model (from config.json or the Mod Options menu).
[*]What you did, what you expected, what happened instead.
[/olist]

[b]Longer logs[/b] don't fit in Steam comments. Two good roads:
[list]
[*]Best: open an issue at [url=https://github.com/TraxData313/ImmersiveAI/issues]github.com/TraxData313/ImmersiveAI/issues[/url] — you can attach the whole log.txt as a file there, and the report stays findable for the next person with the same problem.
[*]Steam-only: paste it at [url=https://privatebin.net/]privatebin.net[/url] and post the link here. Set the expiration to [b]1 week[/b] — shorter and the link may die before I get to it.
[/list]

Known quirks already on the list:
[list]
[*]In the face-to-face panel, a slow reply can briefly show "..." — click "(wait for them to answer)" again and the words arrive. The chat window (hotkey O) doesn't have this. Local models stretch the wait a lot — prefer the chat window when running local.
[/list]

One rule: keep your API key out of screenshots and pastes. I will never ask for it.
