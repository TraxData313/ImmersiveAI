An advanced roleplaying and relationship mod. I built it as an AI engineer who knows what modern LLMs can actually do when you stop prompt-bombing them and start treating them right.

[b][size=4]Looking for...?[/size][/b]
If one of these searches brought you here - yes, this is that mod:

[list]
[*][b]AI dialogue[/b] in Bannerlord - talk to any NPC through the AI you choose, including a [b]free[/b] Gemini key
[*]NPCs with [b]real long-term memory[/b] - they remember you across conversations
[*]NPCs that [b]approach you[/b] and [b]write you letters[/b] on their own
[*]an [b]AI wife or companion[/b] that roleplays and never forgets your story - [b]courted and married in conversation[/b], companions included
[*]asking about [b]game mechanics in-game[/b] instead of alt-tabbing to Google
[/list]

[b]The NPCs are alive:[/b]
[list]
[*]Distinct voices, no repetition - and when you leave a letter unanswered, they hear the silence and hold their peace.
[*]They grow: recent talks stay word-for-word, older ones settle into one deep memory they rewrite in their own words - the names, promises and debts between you - beside their own sense of self and opinion of you.
[*]Every soul begins as somebody: at your first meeting a private starting truth is written for them - an old wound, an odd habit, a vanity - grown from their real story. Yours to read or rewrite.
[*]They see the moment - time, place, who stands near, what lately happened in the world - and they have moods, down to the women keeping a personal monthly cycle, gently simulated.
[*][b]They remember your battles.[/b] Everyone who fought at your side keeps the day: who downed whom, who bled, what was won - the last in detail, older ones by name ("what happened at the storming of Varcheg?"), from a real chronicle of every fight, sea included.
[*]Your companions witness the everyday road too - the towns, the trade you struck, the men you hired, the tasks you carry and how each ended - and they know their own wounds, and see yours.
[*]They are free: told who they are and what their world is, never forced. Go ahead and break an NPC's mind by transcending it out of the matrix!
[*]They decide when to approach or write first - you set the pace with the on-map SOCIALNESS dial (0 = leave me be).
[/list]

[b][size=4]Court them. Wed them. Hire them.[/size][/b]
[list]
[*][b]Marriage by courtship.[/b] Win a heart in conversation and it walks its own road - liking, love, readiness, betrothal, wedding - moved by their own judgment of your talks, not by a menu.
[*][b]They write their own misgivings:[/b] what truly worries them about a life together, in their own words. Talk them over; only they decide when life has answered one, and their readiness waits until nothing they wrote still stands.
[*]Station guards the hand, not the heart: a great house's daughter may love anyone, but her hand asks a suitor of standing - and her family's blessing costs a bride-price, haggled in talk or letter.
[*][b]Companion brides and grooms[/b] - vanilla forbids them; here they are the point. Wed the wanderer riding at your side and she keeps her place and duties.
[*][b]Hiring by handshake:[/b] agree service and price with a wanderer in the talk itself. They open at their worth and never volunteer their lowest price.
[*]Nothing is sealed by words alone - every bargain, betrothal and wedding takes your confirming click, and the wedding is the real game marriage.
[/list]

[b]And useful - stop googling stuff:[/b]

Ask your scout how to make the party faster, your quartermaster about the stocks, anyone anything about the game - they can quietly search the web mid-reply and answer in their own voice, never citing a wiki. They reach for tools instead of one info-dump prompt: the encyclopedia, a look around, the muster roll, the scales of battle, the market ledger, your chronicle.

[b]Your ways to reach them:[/b]
[list]
[*]Face to face - they see you coming and greet you as you approach.
[*]A chat window (hotkey [b]O[/b]) - quick words with anyone near you, no ceremony.
[*]A letter window (hotkey [b]Y[/b]) - the whole correspondence, couriers riding real in-game days, a desk to write from.
[*]Both windows edit a character's prompt, or the whole world's, [b]without leaving the game[/b] - it speaks from the next reply.
[*]Any language in, the same language out. (The mod's own UI is English for now.)
[/list]

[b][size=4]What you need - and what it costs[/size][/b]

You bring [b]your own API key[/b]. Pick a row:

[list]
[*][b]Just works[/b] - OpenRouter + openai/gpt-5.6-luna, the default the mod is tuned against. [b]openrouter.ai[/b]
[*][b]Free[/b] - Gemini + gemini-3.6-flash: real free tier, no card, ~1,500 replies a day. [b]aistudio.google.com[/b]. Two catches: Google trains on free-tier traffic, and it's slow.
[*][b]Cheapest paid[/b] - DeepSeek + deepseek-v4-flash, about half the default's cost. [b]platform.deepseek.com[/b]
[*][b]Also fine[/b] - OpenAI, Anthropic (Claude), a local model, or any OpenAI-compatible service via the Custom endpoint option.
[/list]

[b]Costs are yours and visible.[/b] A typical exchange runs about a tenth of a cent on the default - $10 covers many thousands of messages, shown as you play, with daily totals and a hard cap. Any model must carry [b]native tool calling[/b] or the NPCs go duller.

[b]Not sure which to pick?[/b] Prices side by side, each provider's catch, and the full local-model setup (models, VRAM, thinking OFF, context): [url=https://github.com/TraxData313/ImmersiveAI/blob/master/docs/choosing-a-model.md]Which AI should I use?[/url] [b]Local models are tinkerers-only, at your own risk[/b] - you own the setup; I don't debug them. For a free taste of the idea first, try the ChatAI mod - it's good; it inspired this one.

[b][size=4]Installation[/size][/b]

[list=1]
[*]Vortex: install as usual. Manual: extract the zip into your game's Modules folder, so you get Modules\ImmersiveAI\SubModule.xml.
[*]Get a key at [b]openrouter.ai[/b] and add a little credit ($5-10 goes a long way), or a free one at [b]aistudio.google.com[/b]. Running a local model? Skip this step.
[*]Enable [b]Immersive AI[/b] and start the game once - it creates Documents\...\Configs\ImmersiveAI\config.json and tells you exactly where to paste the key. Paste it (or use Mod Options with MCM), pick your backend, restart.
[/list]

[b][size=4]If characters are silent[/size][/b]
[list]
[*][b]Read the startup message.[/b] It says what it found ("connected", a missing/wrong key, a network problem) and where to fix it. Fix config.json and restart; the check runs again.
[*][b]Check the log:[/b] Configs\ImmersiveAI\log.txt records every call and error in plain words. A wrong or dead key shows ONE amber notice, then goes quiet instead of erroring hourly. Still stuck? Post its last lines in the comments - the mod never logs your key.
[/list]

[b][size=4]Privacy[/size][/b]
[list]
[*]Your conversations - plus the world context around them (character sheets, campaign facts, your name) - go to the AI provider [b]you[/b] configure, under [b]your[/b] key and their terms, nowhere else. Their policy is theirs: notably, Google's [b]free[/b] tier trains on what it receives. (Local: nothing leaves your machine.)
[*]With web search on (default), look-ups go to DuckDuckGo - turn it off to keep everything with your provider.
[*][b]No telemetry whatsoever.[/b] All memories live as plain files on your own disk, yours to read, edit, or delete.
[*][b]AI content disclosure:[/b] all character dialogue is generated at play time by the AI model you configure.
[/list]

[b][size=4]Compatibility & shaping[/size][/b]
[list]
[*]Bannerlord v1.4.7, new campaigns and existing saves alike. One light Harmony patch (bundled), no vanilla behavior altered - it coexists with overhauls, War Sails, Marry Anyone and Training Battles. MCM optional; without it, everything is in config.json.
[*][b]Safe to remove mid-campaign.[/b] Saves load fine without the mod (just the usual "different modules" warning), and memories live outside them - reinstall later and every character remembers you.
[*]A clean-room rewrite inspired by the ChatAi mod (studied via decompilation only - no code copied). Fully original source.
[*]Under Documents\...\Configs\ImmersiveAI\: global_prompt.txt colors every mind in the world, and each character's folder holds their own prompt (in their own voice - "I stutter when I am nervous"), memories, self-image, battles and letters as readable text.
[/list]
