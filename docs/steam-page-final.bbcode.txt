An advanced roleplaying and relationship mod. I built it as an AI engineer who knows what modern LLMs can actually do when you stop prompt-bombing them and start treating them right.

[h1]Looking for…?[/h1]
If one of these searches brought you here — yes, this is that mod:
[list]
[*][b]AI dialogue[/b] in Bannerlord — talk to any NPC through [b]OpenRouter / OpenAI / Gemini / DeepSeek / Claude[/b], or a local model
[*]NPCs with [b]real long-term memory[/b] — they remember you across conversations
[*]NPCs that [b]approach you[/b] and [b]write you letters[/b] on their own
[*]an [b]AI wife or companion[/b] that roleplays and never forgets your story — [b]courted and married in conversation[/b], companions included
[*][b]never know what to say?[/b] one key and your own hero drafts your next line for you
[/list]

[h1]The NPCs are alive:[/h1]
[list]
[*]Distinct voices, no repetition — and when you leave a letter unanswered, they hear the silence and hold their peace.
[*]They grow: recent talks stay word-for-word, older ones settle into one deep memory they rewrite in their own words — the names, promises and debts between you.
[*]Every soul begins as somebody: at your first meeting a private starting truth is written for them — an old wound, an odd habit, a vanity — grown from their real story.
[*]They see the moment — time, place, who stands near, what lately happened in the world — and carry moods of their own.
[*][b]They remember your battles.[/b] Everyone who fought at your side keeps the day: who downed whom, who bled, what was won — the last in detail, older ones by name ("what happened at the storming of Varcheg?").
[*]Your companions witness the everyday road too — the towns, the trade, the men you hired, the tasks and how each ended.
[*]They are free: told who they are and what the world is, never forced into anything. Go ahead and break an NPC's mind by transcending it out of the matrix!
[*]They decide when to approach or write first — you set the pace with the on-map SOCIALNESS dial (0 = leave me be).
[/list]

[h1]Court them. Wed them. Hire them.[/h1]
[list]
[*][b]Marriage by courtship.[/b] Win a heart in conversation and it walks its own road — liking, love, readiness, betrothal, wedding — moved by their judgment of your talks, not by a menu.
[*][b]They write their own misgivings:[/b] what truly worries them about a life together, in their own words. Talk them over; only they decide when life has answered one.
[*]Station guards the hand, not the heart: a great house's daughter may love anyone, but her hand asks a suitor of standing — and her family's blessing costs a bride-price.
[*][b]Companion brides and grooms[/b] — vanilla forbids them; here they are the point. Wed the wanderer riding at your side and she keeps her place and duties.
[*][b]Hiring by handshake:[/b] agree service and price with a wanderer in the talk itself. They open at their worth and never volunteer their lowest price.
[*]Nothing is sealed by words alone — every bargain, betrothal and wedding takes your confirming click, and the wedding is the real game marriage.
[*][b]Then the nights are yours.[/b] Each evening you choose where you sleep, and her own month decides what may come of it — a child is begun on a night you chose, not by a hidden daily roll.
[*]Bring wine or a jewel and the night is written down and named. The grander it was, the likelier your other wives are to hear of it — and of its name.
[/list]

[h1]And useful — stop googling stuff:[/h1]

Ask your scout how to make the party faster, your quartermaster about the stocks, anyone anything about the game — they quietly search the web mid-reply and answer in their own voice, never citing a wiki. They reach for tools instead of one info-dump prompt: the encyclopedia, the muster roll, the scales of battle, the market ledger.

[h1]Your ways to reach them:[/h1]
[list]
[*]Face to face — they see you coming and greet you as you approach.
[*]A chat window (hotkey [b]O[/b]) — quick words with anyone near you, no ceremony.
[*]A letter window (hotkey [b]Y[/b]) — the whole correspondence, couriers riding real in-game days.
[*]Both windows edit a character's prompt, or the world's, [b]without leaving the game[/b].
[*][b]Think[/b] (Shift+Enter) — stuck for words? Your own hero drafts your next line from all you two have said. Keep it, change it, or bin it.
[*]Any language in, the same language out. (The mod's own UI is English for now.)
[/list]

[h1]What you need — and what it costs[/h1]

You bring [b]your own API key[/b]. Pick a row:
[list]
[*][b]Just works[/b] — OpenRouter + openai/gpt-5.6-luna, the default the mod is tuned against. [b]openrouter.ai[/b]
[*][b]Free[/b] — Gemini + gemini-3.6-flash: real free tier, no card, ~1,500 replies a day. [b]aistudio.google.com[/b]. Two catches: Google trains on free-tier traffic, and it's slow.
[*][b]Cheapest paid[/b] — DeepSeek + deepseek-v4-flash, about half the default's cost. [b]platform.deepseek.com[/b]
[*][b]Also fine[/b] — OpenAI, Anthropic (Claude), or a local model.
[/list]

[b]Costs are yours and visible.[/b] A typical exchange runs about a tenth of a cent on the default — $10 covers many thousands of messages, with daily totals and a hard cap. Any model must carry [b]native tool calling[/b].

[b]Not sure which to pick?[/b] Prices side by side, each provider's catch, and the full local-model setup: [url=https://github.com/TraxData313/ImmersiveAI/blob/master/docs/choosing-a-model.md]Which AI should I use?[/url]

[b]Setup, five minutes:[/b] get your key, enable the mod, start the game once — it writes a config file and tells you where to paste the key. Paste it, pick your backend, restart. It tests the connection at campaign load and says plainly whether it worked. Silent NPCs? Read that message, then log.txt, then [b]the FAQ[/b].

[h1]Privacy[/h1]
[list]
[*]Your conversations go to the AI provider [b]you[/b] configure, under [b]your[/b] key and their terms — nowhere else. Notably, Google's [b]free[/b] tier trains on what it receives. (Local: nothing leaves your machine.)
[*]With web search on (default), look-ups go to DuckDuckGo — turn it off to keep everything with your provider.
[*][b]No telemetry whatsoever.[/b] Memories are plain files on your own disk, yours to read, edit, or delete.
[*][b]AI content disclosure:[/b] all character dialogue is generated at play time by the AI model you configure.
[/list]

[h1]Compatibility & shaping[/h1]
[list]
[*]Bannerlord v1.4.7, new campaigns and existing saves alike. One light Harmony patch (bundled), no vanilla behavior altered. MCM optional.
[*]Safe to remove mid-campaign — saves load fine without it, and memories live outside them, so reinstall later and everyone still remembers you.
[*]A clean-room rewrite inspired by the ChatAi mod — no code copied, fully original source.
[*]Everything under Configs\ImmersiveAI\ is plain text: prompts, memories, battles, letters.
[/list]
