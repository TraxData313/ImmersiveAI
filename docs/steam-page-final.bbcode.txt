An advanced roleplaying and relationship mod. I built it as an AI engineer who knows what modern LLMs can actually do when you stop prompt-bombing them and start treating them right.

[h1]Looking for…?[/h1] 
If one of these searches brought you here — yes, this is that mod:
[list]
[*][b]AI dialogue[/b] in Bannerlord - talk to any NPC through [b]OpenRouter (recommended) / OpenAI / Claude[/b], or a local model if you like to tinker
[*]NPCs with [b]real long-term memory[/b] — they remember you across conversations
[*]NPCs that [b]approach you[/b] and [b]write you letters[/b] on their own
[*]an [b]AI wife or companion[/b] that roleplays and never forgets your story
[*]asking about [b]game mechanics in-game[/b] instead of alt-tabbing to Google
[/list]

[h1]The NPCs are alive:[/h1]
[list]
[*]Distinct voices, no repetition — and when you leave a letter unanswered, they hear the silence and hold their peace.
[*]They evolve: recent talks stay word-for-word, old ones settle into deep memory, and they keep their own goals, lasting truths, and opinion of you.
[*]They see the moment — time, place, who stands near, what lately happened in the world.
[*]They have moods — down to the women keeping a personal monthly cycle, gently simulated.
[*]Your wife remembers your whole story. She comes to you, or writes when you're away — a real wife waiting for her warrior. She will roleplay with you.
[*]They are free: told who they are and what the world is, never forced into anything. Go ahead and break an NPC's mind by transcending it out of the matrix!
[*]They decide when to approach or write first — you set the pace with the on-map SOCIALNESS dial (0 = leave me be).
[/list]

[h1]And useful — stop googling stuff:[/h1]
[list]
[*]Ask your scout how to make the party faster; ask your quartermaster about the stocks.
[*]Ask anything about the game — they can quietly search the web mid-reply and answer in their own voice, never citing a wiki.
[*]They reach for tools instead of one mega info-dump prompt: the encyclopedia, a look around, the muster roll, the scales of battle, the market ledger, their own truths and goals.
[/list]

[h1]Your ways to reach them:[/h1]
[list]
[*]Face to face — they see you coming and greet you as you approach.
[*]A chat window (hotkey [b]O[/b]) — quick words with anyone near you, no ceremony.
[*]A letter window (hotkey [b]Y[/b]) — the whole correspondence, couriers riding real in-game days, and a desk to write from.
[*]Any language in, the same language out. (The mod's own UI is English for now.)
[/list]

[h1]What you need — and what it costs[/h1]

You bring [b]your own API key[/b]. Recommended, in order:

[olist]
[*][b]OpenRouter with openai/gpt-5.6-luna[/b] — the default, what the mod is tuned and tested against. One key from [b]openrouter.ai[/b] reaches every model at the providers' own prices. [b]openai/gpt-5.4-mini[/b] is the cheaper fallback, also tested.
[*][b]OpenAI[/b] with those same two models.
[*][b]Anthropic (Claude)[/b] — works, haiku-4.5 by default; not tested at length.
[/olist]

Any other model, from the dropdown or typed in yourself, is at your own risk, and must carry [b]native tool calling[/b] or the NPCs go noticeably duller. This mod does [b]not[/b] use the "free" Player2 backend — the tool calling it is built on would need a paid tier there anyway. For a free taste first, try the ChatAI mod — it's good; it inspired this one.

[b]Local models: tinkerers only, at your own risk.[/b] The [b]Local[/b] backend (LM Studio / Ollama) exists because people asked, not because it plays well. You own the setup: an instruct model with [b]native tool calling[/b], [b]thinking/reasoning OFF[/b] in your server (a thinking model burns its whole budget in silence and answers "..."), a 12–16+ GB GPU, and patience. Details in the pinned FAQ. I don't debug local setups.

[b]Costs are yours and visible.[/b] A typical exchange is around a cent or less on the recommended models — $10 covers thousands of messages. Each interaction shows its tokens and price as you play, with daily totals and an optional hard cap.

[h1]Quick setup (five minutes)[/h1]

[olist]
[*]Get a key at [b]openrouter.ai[/b] (recommended — one key, many models) or [b]platform.openai.com[/b] / [b]console.anthropic.com[/b], and add a little credit ($5–10 goes a long way). Local users skip this step.
[*]Install, enable [b]Immersive AI[/b] in the launcher, start the game once — it creates its config file and tells you where to paste the key.
[*]Paste the key into config.json (or the Mod Options menu with MCM), pick your backend, restart.
[*]The startup check says whether it worked — then go talk to somebody.
[/olist]

[h1]Privacy[/h1]
[list]
[*]Your conversations and the world context around them go to the AI provider [b]you[/b] configure, under [b]your[/b] key and their terms. Nowhere else. (Local backend: nothing leaves your machine.)
[*]With web search on (default), characters may look things up mid-reply; those queries go to DuckDuckGo — turn it off to keep everything with your provider.
[*][b]No telemetry whatsoever.[/b] All memories are plain files on your own disk, yours to read, edit, or delete.
[/list]

[h1]AI content disclosure[/h1]

All character dialogue in this mod is generated at play time by the AI model you configure.

[h1]Removing the mod mid-campaign[/h1]

Safe to remove at any time — saves load fine without it (just the usual "different modules" warning). Memories live outside the saves as plain files, so reinstall later and every character still remembers you.

[h1]If characters are silent[/h1]
[list]
[*][b]Read the startup message.[/b] On campaign load the mod tests your connection and says what it found and where to fix it. Fix config.json, restart, and the check runs again.
[*][b]Check log.txt[/b] in the config folder — every call and error in plain words. Still stuck? Post its last lines; it never contains your key.
[/list]

[h1]Shape your world (the prompt files)[/h1]

Under Documents\Mount and Blade II Bannerlord\Configs\ImmersiveAI\:
[list]
[*]global_prompt.txt — a few sentences that color every mind in the world ("the year is one of famine").
[*]NPCs\campaign_*\<character>\custom_instructions.txt — per-character secrets and quirks. That folder also holds their memories, self-image, goals and correspondence as readable text.
[/list]

Changes apply on the next conversation. No restart.
